# Service card artwork

The category images live in `public/images/services/` and are served locally as WebP assets. Home cards use decorative images; the visible category names provide the accessible button names.

`mlbb.webp` is the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang application artwork from MOONTON's [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/uz/app/mobile-legends-bang-bang/id1160056295), downloaded on 2026-10-27. The source URL is:

https://is1-ssl.mzstatic.com/image/thumb/Purple221/v4/d9/88/76/d9887647-00fd-09c8-8a61-c6c31a1bfe2a/AppIcon-0-0-1x_U007emarketing-0-7-0-85-220.png/512x512bb.webp

The other five images were created with the built-in ImageGen tool, then exported at 640 pixels wide as WebP for mobile delivery. These are illustrative service images, not screenshots or account listings.

The final prompts share this prefix:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium service category thumbnail for VAYNEXO Telegram mini app.

Subjects inserted after the prefix:

- `marketplace.webp`: Two premium fantasy game account collectible cards, one in front with a masked blue-armored game hero portrait, a small golden exchange medallion and silver clasp. Account marketplace.
- `boost.webp`: An ornate high rank esports crest with silver wings and a glowing violet crystal at its heart, two elegant battle blades behind it. Rank boost service.
- `rental.webp`: A sophisticated chrome key floating in front of a translucent teal game account card and a small glass clock. Temporary game account rental.
- `telegram.webp`: A recognizable three-dimensional Telegram paper plane made of frosted cyan glass with two polished golden five-point Stars and a small violet Premium star. Telegram Stars and Premium services.
- `coach.webp`: An elegant intelligent humanoid AI head in polished dark chrome and luminous cyan glass, fine visible neural filaments inside the head, violet neural halo. AI chat assistant.

Each prompt ends with:

> Single still-life cinematic 3D product render, sophisticated realistic materials, not cartoon. Landscape composition 3:2. One clear centered subject, big and immediately recognizable at 160px thumbnail, generous 15 percent framing. Deep midnight navy studio background #101e34, subtle cyan rim light and restrained blue reflections. Crisp premium detail, luminous but controlled. No text, letters, UI, borders, badges, watermarks or people. No card layout outside the subject.
